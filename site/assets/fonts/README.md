Self-hosted subsets (latin + latin-ext, covers all Czech diacritics) of Google Fonts families, all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1:
Schibsted Grotesk, JetBrains Mono, Bricolage Grotesque, Instrument Serif, IBM Plex Sans, Fraunces, Hanken Grotesk.
Self-hosting avoids sending visitor IPs to Google (GDPR, cf. LG München I, 3 O 17493/20) and saves a DNS/TLS round trip on mobile.
Regenerate with `tools/fetch_fonts.py`.
